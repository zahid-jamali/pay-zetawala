## 1. Philippines (PH)

| Authority / Agency | Rate / Table | Typical Revision Cycle | Next Anticipated Review | Where to Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Social Security System (SSS)** | Contribution Schedule (Rate, MSC brackets, EC, MPF) | Every 2 years historically under RA 11199; Jan 2025 (15%) was the final statutory step. Future increases require legislative amendment. | January (if amended by Congress) | https://www.sss.gov.ph (Laws & Circulars section) |
| **Social Security System (SSS)** | Salary Loan Guidelines & Interest Rates | Policy reviews by Social Security Commission (SSC) every 2–3 years. | Ad-hoc (monitored semi-annually) | https://www.sss.gov.ph (Circulars) |
| **Social Security System (SSS)** | Pension Formulas & Minimum Benefits | Statutory (RA 11199). Additional benefits require Presidential Executive Order. | Ad-hoc | https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/ |
| **PhilHealth** | Premium Rate, Income Floor, Ceiling | Universal Health Care Act (RA 11223) capped at 5.0% in 2024–2025. | January annually (check for any floor/ceiling adjustment) | https://www.philhealth.gov.ph (Circulars & Advisories) |
| **Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF)** | Maximum Fund Salary (MFS) & Contribution Rates | HDMF Board of Trustees resolution. Increased from ₱5k to ₱10k in Feb 2024. Stable for 3–5 years. | January / February | https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph (Circulars) |
| **Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)** | Withholding Tax on Compensation & ₱90k 13th-Month Cap | Legislative (Tax Reform Acts by Congress). Rates set under TRAIN Law remain stable. | January annually (for any revenue regulations) | https://www.bir.gov.ph (Tax Tables & Revenue Regulations) |
| **Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE)** | 13th Month Pay Guidelines | Annually in October or November (Labor Advisory confirming Dec 24 deadline and rules). | October / November annually | https://www.dole.gov.ph (Labor Advisories) |
